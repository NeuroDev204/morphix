ffmpeg.exe la ban ffmpeg gon cua Morphix (FFmpeg 8.1.3), dung tu ma nguon bang scripts/build-ffmpeg-slim-windows.sh, lien ket tinh hoan toan.
Cau hinh GPL (GPL-3.0-or-later): co libx264, libvpx, libopus, libmp3lame, libvorbis, libdav1d va zlib.
Ma nguon va giay phep: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html va https://github.com/NeuroDev204/morphix/releases/tag/ffmpeg-8.1.3-morphix.1 (BUILD-INFO.txt trong goi liet ke nguon va SHA256 cua tung thanh phan).
Morphix chi goi ffmpeg nhu mot chuong trinh rieng, khong lien ket thu vien.
